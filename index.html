Puffspire

A one-tap cloud-stacking game. Drop clouds, build your tower, and reach beyond the sky.

How to Play

Tap to release a moving cloud. Align it with the tower below to keep building. Accurate drops help you maintain your tower and improve your score.

Explore Classic, 60S Rush, Challenge, and Daily Tower from the game’s home screen.
