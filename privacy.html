Privacy Policy for Puffspire

Last updated: September 23, 2026

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how the Puffspire mobile game handles information. Puffspire does not require an account to play.

2. Game Data Stored on Your Device

Puffspire stores game progress, scores, unlocked content, challenge results, and preferences such as music, sound, and haptics locally on your device. This data is used to provide gameplay and remember your settings. The game does not send this locally stored gameplay data to us.

3. Advertising and Tracking

The current version of Puffspire does not display third-party advertisements, request App Tracking Transparency permission, access the advertising identifier (IDFA), or use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

4. Support Emails

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use it only to respond to your request and handle related support matters. Please do not send sensitive personal information.

5. Sharing and Retention

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share locally stored gameplay data with advertisers. Support emails may be handled by our email provider and are retained only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records, or comply with applicable law.

6. Your Choices

You can change game preferences in the app. Deleting Puffspire generally removes its locally stored data from that device, although data included in a device backup may be restored later. To ask about a support email you sent, contact us using the address below.

7. This Website

These support and privacy pages do not include advertising or analytics scripts. The provider hosting this website may process standard connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the pages and maintain its service.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email 704362412@qq.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy if Puffspire’s features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
