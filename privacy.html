Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puffspire (the “App,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you play the game or contact the developer.

1. Information We Handle

Game data stored on your device. Puffspire stores progress, best scores, unlocked content, challenge and daily results, tutorial status, and settings such as music, sound, and haptics using local device storage. The App does not require an account.

Device, advertising, and technical data. When advertising, analytics, or security services are active, we and our service providers may process device identifiers (such as IDFV and, with permission, IDFA), IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, device model, operating system, language, app version, network information, advertising interactions, product interactions, gameplay or usage events, and crash, diagnostic, performance, fraud-prevention, or security signals.

Advertising configuration. The App sends its bundle identifier to our configuration service to determine which advertising providers and placements are enabled.

Support communications. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include so that we can respond.

We do not ask for your name, account password, payment-card details, contacts, photos, health data, precise GPS location, or the contents of your files.

2. App Tracking Transparency and IDFA

Puffspire uses Apple’s AppTrackingTransparency framework to request your permission before accessing the advertising identifier or allowing tracking as defined by Apple. If you allow tracking, advertising partners may use IDFA and related data to deliver, measure, or personalize advertising and to link activity across apps or websites owned by other companies. If you deny permission, the App does not access IDFA; contextual or non-personalized advertising may still be shown.

You can review or change this choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

3. How Information Is Used

  • Provide gameplay and remember progress and preferences;
  • Retrieve advertising configuration and deliver, limit, personalize where permitted, and measure ads;
  • Understand product interaction, diagnose crashes, and improve reliability;
  • Detect fraud, abuse, automation, and security threats;
  • Comply with law and valid legal requests; and
  • Respond to support, privacy, and deletion requests.

4. Advertising, Analytics, and Security Providers

Depending on the configuration returned to the App, Puffspire may initialize one or more of the following advertising services. These providers may process device identifiers, advertising data, usage data, coarse location, diagnostics, and network information under their own policies:

  • Chartboost Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Privacy Policy (including Yandex Mobile Ads and related AppMetrica technology)
  • Liftoff / Vungle Privacy Policy
  • InMobi Privacy Policy

The App also contains security and anti-fraud technology that may process technical device or network signals to protect the App and advertising ecosystem. A provider may act as an independent controller for some processing. Its policy governs its own collection, retention, international transfer, and rights procedures.

5. Sharing, Sale, and Targeted Advertising

We may disclose information to the service providers described above for app functionality, advertising, analytics, diagnostics, security, and fraud prevention, and when required by law, safety, or a business transfer. We do not sell personal information for money. Some privacy laws may treat personalized advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising as a “sale,” “sharing,” or “targeted advertising.” You can opt out by denying tracking permission and using choices provided by the relevant advertising partner.

6. Data Retention and Security

Local game data remains on your device until it is reset or the App is removed, subject to device backups. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, and comply with law. Advertising, analytics, and security providers determine their own retention periods under their policies. We use reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Tracking: deny or change tracking permission in iOS Settings.
  • Local game data: deleting the App ordinarily removes locally stored Puffspire data from that device; data restored from a backup may reappear.
  • Advertising choices: use opt-out controls offered by Apple and the advertising providers.
  • Privacy requests: email us to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or an appeal where applicable. Because Puffspire has no account, we may be unable to identify data held only under a technical identifier.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling, withdraw consent, and complain to a data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising applicable rights.

8. International Processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, they use recognized safeguards for international transfers. Laws and privacy protections may differ between jurisdictions.

9. Children’s Privacy

Puffspire is not directed to children under 13, or under the minimum digital-consent age required in the user’s jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to Puffspire, its providers, or applicable law. The revised version will be posted with a new effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the App where appropriate.
